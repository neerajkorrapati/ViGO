ViGo

Verified Student Carpooling and Ride Sharing for VIT Vellore

ViGo is an innovative carpooling network application specifically engineered to solve transit challenges faced by students at VIT Vellore. The platform offers streamlined, real-time carpool party allocation for autos and cabs heading to or from the campus, railway stations, and airports.

Featuring automated, secure verification and seamless integration via native custom WhatsApp group communication links, ViGo enables students to securely connect, share travel costs, split bookings, and coordinate rides efficiently.
